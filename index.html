Before you order+

10 answers about designs, gifts, pricing & timing

Good questions

Before
you order

What kinds of custom items can you make?

3D toys and figures from drawings, written ideas, or photos; busts, statues, cosplay props, and gaming accessories. Plus custom apparel, tumblers and mugs, hats and bags, keychains, engraving services, custom plates, signs and decor, event pieces, and holiday keepsakes.

Can you turn my child’s drawing or words into 3D?

Yes. Send a clear photo of the drawing, or write out the idea. Monsters, superheroes, pets, dinosaurs, robots, fairy characters, and other original creations can become detailed, full-color figures. Tell Symz the size and colors you have in mind; you’ll see a design preview before printing.

Can I request a design based on my own idea?

Yes, that's most of what comes through the door. A sketch, a screenshot, a photo of a person, or a description is enough to start.

Can I order engraving or my own merch design?

Yes. Request custom apparel, tumblers and mugs, hats and bags, keychains, custom plates, signs and decor, or engraving. Include your design, wording, quantity, and the item you want customized so Symz can quote the work.

How much does custom work cost?

Every project is quoted around the item, size, detail, finish, and quantity. Share your idea and budget through the request form, call 848-336-0069, or message @symzcustomz84 for a quote.

What should I include in a custom order?

What you want, roughly how big, colors, any names or dates that go on it, reference photos, drawings, or written ideas, and the date you need it by. If it's for an event, say which one.

Do these work as gifts and for special occasions?

Yes. Weddings, baby showers, anniversaries, custom gift bags, gender reveals, birthday gifts, holiday creations, baby plaques, and graduation pieces can be built around your names, dates, and designs.

What comes in a sip & paint kit?

A custom figure printed in white and ready to paint, 8 paint colors, 4 brushes, a mini drink to sip (swapped for prosecco, rosé, or water to fit the occasion), and a SYMZ thank-you card, all in a gift-ready bag. They make great party favors, and bulk orders are welcome.

How long does an order take?

It depends on size and finish. Small pieces move fast; full-size cosplay builds print in many sections and take longer. Share your date up front and you'll get a straight answer on timing.

Why work with a pro instead of printing it myself?

Modeling, slicing, splitting big parts, sanding seams, and painting is where pieces go right or wrong. You get a finished piece instead of a raw print.
